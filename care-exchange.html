Ethical Standards

Caregiver Dignity & Volunteer Code of Conduct

Our community thrives on trust, empathy, and absolute clarity of non-clinical boundaries.

Zero Clinical Intervention

Helpers never administer intravenous lines, injections, wound dressings, or alter prescription dosages. We provide gentle companion supervision and non-clinical warmth only.

Dietary & Cultural Sanctity

Strict adherence to vegetarian, Jain, or halal dietary practices in the recipient's home. Zero outside food without explicit consent from the primary family caregiver.

Sovereign Privacy

Never take photographs, record videos, or discuss the elder's cognitive condition with outsiders. Patient dignity and household confidentiality are legally protected.

Punctuality Commitment

Elderly family members experience acute anxiety with unexpected delays. Volunteer helpers arrive 5 minutes early or notify the family via the verified circle phone immediately.

Good Samaritan Mutual Waiver

Non-Clinical Care Stewardship Agreement

1. Non-Clinical Scope of Assistance: The undersigned volunteer caregiver acknowledges that assistance provided through KinTime consists strictly of non-medical respite, companionship, and neighborhood errands. Under no circumstances shall the helper perform invasive medical procedures, dispense controlled medications without prescription authorization, or assume licensed nurse liability.

2. Good Samaritan Protection (Indian Legal Precedents): Both parties voluntarily enter this exchange under Good Samaritan legal protections recognized by the Supreme Court of India and Section 134A of the Motor Vehicles Act. No monetary consideration or cash is exchanged; all compensation occurs strictly via immutable hour credits in the KinTime double-entry ledger.

3. Escrow Lock Authorization: Upon executing this claim, the escrow lock will register on the immutable ledger. When the respite session is completed, both parties verify completion to release the credit.
