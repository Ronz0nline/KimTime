The Three Inviolable Ledger Laws

Why time-banking fundamentally protects families from financial exploitation and inflation.

Absolute 1:1 Equality

One hour of human life is worth exactly one hour of human life. A software engineer's hour is mathematically equivalent to a student's or a homemaker's hour. Time cannot be devalued.

Pre-Funded Escrow Lock

Whenever an assistance request is published, hours are locked in cryptographic escrow. When a volunteer completes the session, payment settles instantaneously without human negotiation.

Zero Interest, Zero Debt

Negative balances never accrue interest, fees, or penalties. Overdrafts are safely backed by community donations from the Angel Pool, so caregivers are never financially trapped.
