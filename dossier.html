Legal & Sovereign Architecture

Statutory Legal Protections

KinTime operates under explicit non-clinical mutual aid frameworks validated by Indian statutes and Supreme Court directives.

Good Samaritan Shield

Protected under Section 134A of the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019 and Supreme Court of India Good Samaritan guidelines. Voluntary helpers acting in good faith bear zero civil or criminal liability.

Senior Citizens Act 2007

Aligned with the Maintenance & Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens (MWPSC) Act 2007, fostering community-led elder protection, dignity preservation, and neighborhood social safety nets.

DPDP Act Privacy

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Recipient medical dossiers are encrypted end-to-end, never shared with third parties, and accessible only upon mutual task acceptance.
