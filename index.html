The Economic Loop

How KinTime Works

Time-banking replaces cash barriers with an accountable circle of mutual reliance. Visually breaking down the loop of depositing and withdrawing time.

01

Secure Your Identity

Safety is our absolute priority. Before joining, every member passes a strict identity and vulnerability sector background check via Aadhaar / DigiLocker and signs a Good Samaritan Mutual Waiver.

02

Deposit Your Time

Pick up Jan Aushadhi prescriptions, drop off a warm meal, or read to an elder neighbor. For every hour you spend helping a neighbor, exactly 1.0 hour is deposited into your digital Passbook.

03

Withdraw Relief

When you are exhausted, sick, or need to attend your own medical appointment, spend your earned banked hours to request a verified neighbor to cover for your family.

04

The Care Grant

Burnout doesn't wait until you have a positive balance. Every new family receives an immediate 5-hour Starter Care Grant upon verification to access emergency relief on day one.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding trust, safety, liability waivers, and the double-entry care ledger.

What happens if I am too burned out to earn hours before I need help?

To solve the Caregiver Liquidity Paradox, every new family receives an immediate 5-Hour Starter Care Grant upon identity verification. Furthermore, if your balance reaches zero, the system allows you to access a 3-Hour Community Overdraft funded by volunteer angel hours.

How do I know the person coming to my house is safe?

KinTime does not allow anonymous accounts. Every user must pass a strict identity verification check (DigiLocker / Aadhaar) and complete a Care Dossier before interacting with Tier 2 in-home requests. Every completed session is mutually reviewed.

What if there is a medical emergency while I am providing respite?

As a volunteer companion, your role is strictly non-clinical supervision, not medical intervention. If an emergency occurs, your directive under the Good Samaritan Protocol is to immediately call 112 / emergency services and then alert the family physician listed in their Care Dossier.
